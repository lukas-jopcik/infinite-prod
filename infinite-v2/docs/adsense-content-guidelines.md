# AdSense Content Enhancement Guidelines

## Overview

This document provides actionable guidelines for enhancing content quality to meet AdSense requirements. Based on the audit findings, several pages have thin content warnings that need to be addressed.

## AdSense Content Quality Requirements

### Minimum Content Standards
- **Word Count**: Minimum 500 words per page (recommended 800+ words)
- **Originality**: 100% original content, no duplicate or scraped content
- **Value**: Content must provide genuine value to users
- **Readability**: Clear structure with headings, paragraphs, and proper formatting
- **Relevance**: Content must be relevant to the page topic and user intent

### Content Types That Perform Well
- Educational articles with detailed explanations
- How-to guides and tutorials
- In-depth analysis and research
- Original insights and commentary
- Comprehensive resource lists
- Interactive content and tools

## Current Issues Identified

### Pages Requiring Enhancement

Based on the AdSense audit, the following areas need content enhancement:

1. **Article Pages** - Some articles may be too brief
2. **Category Pages** - Need introductory content and context
3. **About Page** - Should be more comprehensive
4. **Search Results** - Need better empty states and guidance

## Content Enhancement Strategies

### 1. Article Pages Enhancement

#### Current Structure Issues:
- Brief descriptions without context
- Missing background information
- Lack of related topics discussion

#### Enhancement Template:
```markdown
# [Article Title]

## Introduction (100-150 words)
- Brief overview of the topic
- Why this matters to readers
- What they'll learn

## Background Context (200-300 words)
- Historical context
- Scientific background
- Related discoveries or events

## Main Content (300-500 words)
- Detailed explanation
- Key facts and figures
- Visual descriptions

## Implications and Significance (150-200 words)
- Why this discovery matters
- Future implications
- Impact on science/astronomy

## Related Topics (100-150 words)
- Connections to other discoveries
- Further reading suggestions
- Related articles on the site

## Conclusion (50-100 words)
- Summary of key points
- Call to action for engagement
```

#### AI-Generated Content Suggestions:

**For Space Discovery Articles:**
- Add historical context about the discovery method
- Include information about the telescope or instrument used
- Explain the scientific significance
- Add details about the celestial object's properties
- Include comparison with similar objects
- Add information about future observations planned

**For Astronomy News:**
- Provide background on the research team
- Explain the methodology used
- Add context about previous related research
- Include quotes from scientists (if available)
- Add information about the publication or journal
- Explain the peer-review process

## Content Quality Checklist

### Before Publishing:
- [ ] Word count meets minimum requirements (500+ words)
- [ ] Content is 100% original
- [ ] Proper heading structure (H1, H2, H3)
- [ ] Images have descriptive alt text
- [ ] Links are relevant and functional
- [ ] Content provides genuine value
- [ ] No duplicate content from other sources
- [ ] Proper grammar and spelling
- [ ] Mobile-friendly formatting
- [ ] Fast loading times

### SEO Optimization:
- [ ] Target keyword in title and headings
- [ ] Meta description under 160 characters
- [ ] Internal links to related content
- [ ] External links to authoritative sources
- [ ] Proper URL structure
- [ ] Schema markup where applicable

## AI Content Generation Guidelines

### When Using AI for Content Enhancement:

1. **Always Review and Edit**: AI-generated content should be reviewed, fact-checked, and edited
2. **Add Personal Touch**: Include original insights, opinions, or local context
3. **Verify Facts**: Double-check all scientific facts and figures
4. **Maintain Voice**: Ensure content matches the site's tone and style
5. **Add Value**: Don't just expand for word count - add genuine value

### Recommended AI Prompts for Content Enhancement:

**For Article Expansion:**
```

Add: historical context, scientific significance, related discoveries, and future implications. 
Keep the Slovak language and educational tone.

```

## Best Practices

### Content Creation:
1. **Research First**: Always research thoroughly before writing
2. **Cite Sources**: Provide proper attribution for facts and images
3. **Update Regularly**: Keep content current and accurate
4. **User Feedback**: Listen to user comments and suggestions
5. **Quality Over Quantity**: Better to have fewer high-quality pages

### AdSense Compliance:
1. **No Clickbait**: Avoid misleading titles and descriptions
2. **No Duplicate Content**: Each page must be unique
3. **Proper Attribution**: Always credit sources and images
4. **User Value**: Focus on providing genuine value to users
5. **Regular Updates**: Keep content fresh and relevant
